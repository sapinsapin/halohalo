## Where things stand, in one screen

**ASR.** `whisper-large-v3-pld-<lang>-norm` is the recommended model for nine
languages (CER 4.6–16.0 %, WER 12.2–30.7 %, frozen speaker- and prompt-disjoint
split, normalised text), all published. English trained wrong and is held back.
omni-7B matched omni-1B (scale bought nothing); a 4-gram LM over PLD's own
transcripts lowers omni-1B's WER by 4.5–6.7 points; web text made the LM worse.
Twelve WER points of every earlier number were orthography — PLD's stress
accents and punctuation — which is now a convention (`--normalise`), not a
hidden cost. Details: `docs/bakeoff_r1_results.md`, `docs/asr_decoder_plan.md`.

**TTS.** Orpheus-3B with a character frontend is intelligible in nine
languages and gives Tausug its first TTS. Whether Orpheus beats Meta's MMS-TTS depends on the judge: our PLD-trained judges say yes in seven of nine languages, Meta's MMS-1b-all says no in seven. Each plausibly favours its own family's audio, so that comparison is unresolved until people listen.; nine adapters are published.
Pangasinan has too little data for it. The untrained **Qwen3-TTS base is the
best voice match measured on anything** (speaker similarity 0.77), and our
finetune of it is broken. The SpeechT5 fleet is private. Details:
`docs/tts_sota_plan.md` §6.

**Hub and Space.** 27 cards rewritten with a plain-language opening and a
"use this instead" pointer; the syllable CTC arms deleted; the dashboard marks
★ the model to try per language and opens Transcribe on it.

**Infrastructure.** One cloud VM (`halohalo-rtx6000-b`, 1 TB, stopped), with
an idle watchdog that powers it off after 30 minutes of nothing.

## What the RTX 3070 can and cannot do

8 GB, of which ~6 GB is free with the desktop running. Measured on it: the
whisper-small fleet trained at batch 8 in **12–16 minutes per 500 steps**, so
about an hour a language; the first dataset load for a language is ~13 minutes
(single-process — multiprocess map over audio deadlocks on WSL's 9p mounts),
cached after that. Only Cebuano and Kapampangan are cached locally so far.

| fits | does not fit |
|---|---|
| inference with anything up to ~2B parameters in fp16/bf16 | training whisper-large-v3 (1.55B full finetune ≈ 25 GB) |
| training whisper-small (242M) — the whole original fleet was trained here | training omni-1B/7B CTC |
| training Qwen3-TTS **0.6B** and w2v-BERT (600M) with 8-bit Adam, small batches | training Qwen3-TTS 1.7B |
| training omni-300M CTC (cached locally) with 8-bit Adam + checkpointing | Orpheus-3B at useful speed (QLoRA fits; ~2 min a step, measured) |
| CPU work: LM building, decoding, scoring saved hypotheses | Fish S2 Pro (4B + 0.4B), D3 with an unfrozen 1B encoder, D4 |

**Disk rule for every local job.** C: has 12 GB free and a full C: took WSL
down three times on 2026-09-17. WSL's own filesystem lives on C: (its
`ext4.vhdx`), so anything written under the WSL home grows C: — including the
pip cache, already 3.9 GB at `~/.cache/pip`. Local jobs keep venvs under
`/mnt/d/halohalo/`, set `PIP_CACHE_DIR=/mnt/d/pip_cache` and
`TMPDIR=/mnt/d/tmp`, and use `HF_HOME=/mnt/d/hf_cache` (already the default in
`.env`). Check `nvidia-smi` first: other sessions have trained on this card.
